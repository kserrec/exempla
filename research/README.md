# Research records

This directory preserves decision evidence outside learner-facing pages.

- `learner-centered-rebuild.json` records the complete retain/remove decision
  and named verification passes for every v1.0.0 entry.
- `learner-centered-cutover-overrides.json` records the explicit path-specific
  learner and eight-part quality prose required where v1 text could not be
  carried over safely.
- `learner-centered-gap-research.json` records the channels, pinned candidate
  inspections, scores, verification passes, admissions, rejections, and honest
  unresolved gaps from the post-cutover search.
- `learner-centered-remediation.json` records the verified 150-path baseline,
  path-identity migration, targeted claim-to-source checks, capacity decisions,
  and the follow-up research for every affected Level 1 gap.
- `learner-centered-audit.json` is the active learner-centered corpus audit. It
  reconciles the accepted set, distributions, identities, score guardrails,
  prerequisites and concepts, pins, live metadata, licenses, generated pages,
  source claims, and neighboring-level comparisons.
- `rejections.json` is append-only: it contains discovery rejections, cutover
  removals, and later serious candidates that did not qualify.
- `audit.json` is the historical v1 Size / Difficulty / Complexity corpus audit;
  it is not the current cutover verification record.
- Accepted evidence lives in schema-version-3 files under `../catalog/` and is
  rendered into `../languages/`.

See the [research process](../docs/research-process.md), [learning-level
rubric](../docs/learning-levels.md), and [quality gate](../docs/qualification.md).
